# Zero-budget App Sales Roadmap

## Operating rule
Build one small useful product at a time. Use free tools and free hosting while validating demand. Never promise guaranteed income. Do not pay for ads, domains, APIs, or subscriptions without the owner's explicit approval.

## Product 01 — ဆိုင်စာရင်း Pro
Target: small retail shops, roadside stalls, and home businesses.
Problem: paper-based stock, daily sales, and customer debt records are easy to lose.
MVP: offline product list, stock deduction, sales, estimated gross profit, debt marking, JSON backup/restore.
Distribution hypothesis: demonstrate the app to local shops and offer a customized branded copy. Validate willingness to pay before expanding.

## Acceptance gates before sale
1. Test add/delete product and stock boundaries.
2. Test cash and debt transactions; repayment must not double-count revenue.
3. Test backup and restore on another browser/device.
4. Test offline after first successful load.
5. Add privacy/data-loss notice and support instructions.
6. Produce release APK only after app ID, icon, signing key ownership, versioning, and install tests are confirmed.
7. Provide buyer a license/usage note and backup guide.

## Not included
Cloud sync, user accounts, multi-device use, tax compliance, receipt printer integration, payment gateway, or guaranteed profit calculations. Profit is an estimate based on entered cost and sale price.

## Sales validation
Collect feedback from 5 potential shop owners. Ask whether they would use it weekly and what is missing. Do not claim customers exist until verified.
